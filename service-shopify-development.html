FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask Toki in the chat or email support@xubitar.com.

Do we own the custom app and code?
Yes. On final payment you own the code, repository and app credentials. We document everything so another developer could pick it up.
Can you work on a store another developer built?
Yes. We start with a short technical review so we understand the existing code before changing it, and we tell you what we find.
Will this slow down my store?
Performance is part of the scope. We test on a staging or development store and check speed before anything goes live.
How do you price custom development?
After a discovery call we send a written scope with a fixed price, timeline and responsibilities. No open-ended hourly surprises.
