FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask Toki in the chat or email support@xubitar.com.

Is it safe to let AI handle customer messages?
We design workflows with approval steps. AI drafts and sorts; your team approves anything customer-facing until you're confident.
Do we need n8n?
No. We pick the simplest reliable tool for the job — n8n, Make, Zapier or custom code — and explain the trade-offs.
Who maintains the workflows?
We document every workflow and can maintain them under a monthly support plan, or hand them over to your team.
How do you measure success?
We agree on a baseline before we start, such as hours spent or response time, and report against it.
