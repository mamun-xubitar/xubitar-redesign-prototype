{{ m.section_head('From brief to design', 'How the design answers the discussion', None) }}
Requirement from the discussionWhere & how it's addressed
Updated services (Shopify development, support, AI automation, SEO/AEO/GEO/LLMO, custom web)Mega menu, home services grid, services overview with a “start from your goal” picker, and one detail page per service with problems, deliverables, process, case study and FAQ.
New product: XocialAISite-wide announcement bar, Products dropdown, home products section and the Products page (copy aligned with xocialai.com).
Niche changeNew positioning (“Reliable engineering for Shopify brands, startups & agencies”) and an About story that frames the shift positively.
Prove reliability and trust, not just codeStore health hero visual, six written commitments, case studies with measured results, client reviews, a clear process and FAQ on ownership and guarantees.
Show store maintenance / renovation details on the landing pageInteractive before/after renovation slider plus a monthly maintenance log, linked to support plans.
Toki chatbot as live chatChat widget on every page with quick replies and human handoff (visual demo — the real widget comes from Toki's embed code).
Target startups, direct clients and agency owners“Who we work with” section on home, Custom web page for startups and a dedicated For agencies partner page.
Market shift: agencies shrinking; work still available from high-end merchants & agenciesWhite-label partnership models (project, monthly hours, overflow), a low-risk pilot task, and a note on NDA work.
Lead capture from the 6-month plan3-step enquiry form collects project type, store URL, problem, timing, budget, role and lead source. Includes booking and a “what happens next” panel.
No promises without evidenceSEO page states “no guaranteed rankings or AI citations”. All numbers, logos and reviews are flagged as sample content to replace with approved data.
{{ m.section_head('Decisions needed', 'Questions for you', 'Answers to these let us replace sample content and finalise the design before development.') }}
  1. Accent colourSignal blue, Emerald or Volt? (Try them in the Review panel.)
  2. Case studies we can publishWhich projects, client names and logos do we have permission to show? Any results or screenshots we can use?
  3. Reviews & ratingsReal client quotes, plus Shopify App Store / Clutch / Google ratings we can link to.
  4. Company numbersYear founded, projects delivered, team size, stores supported, agency partners.
  5. Support plan detailsPrices, hours, response targets, notice period and whether unused hours roll over.
  6. Agency partnership termsMinimum hours, rates, and working hours / time-zone overlap to promise.
  7. “Copyright-related services”The discussion mentions this. We assumed it means copywriting and included it in Custom Web and SEO. Please confirm, or tell us if it's a separate service.
  8. Toki chatbotConfirm the provider and whether human handoff should go to email, Slack or WhatsApp.
  9. Contact detailsOffice address, phone, and whether enquiries go to support@ or a separate sales address (e.g. hello@).
  10. Team photosReal photos and names for the About page — one of the strongest trust signals.
  11. Booking toolCal.com, Calendly or Google Calendar for the “Book a call” button?
  12. Shopify Partner statusCan we show a Shopify Partner badge / Partner Directory profile?

After approval

We collect real content, apply your feedback, then build the pages in the existing Next.js 16 + Tailwind v4 codebase (contact form, careers and admin keep working). The style guide maps every token to the build.
