Agency FAQ

Questions agencies ask us

Will you ever contact our clients directly?
No. We only speak with your clients if you invite us — and then under your agency’s name.
Who owns the code we deliver to clients?
Your agency, or your client — whichever you specify. We transfer repositories and credentials at handoff.
Can you join client calls?
Yes, as part of your team, when it helps. Many partners bring us in for technical discovery.
What time zones do you cover?
To be confirmed by Xubitar — e.g. overlap with UK/EU mornings and US East Coast hours.
How do you estimate work?
We send a written estimate with assumptions and risks, so you can price confidently for your client.
