FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask Toki in the chat or email support@xubitar.com.

How long does a website take?
A focused marketing site typically takes 4–8 weeks from approved scope, depending on content readiness and pages. (Timing to be confirmed by Xubitar.)
Do you write the content?
We can. Many clients give us raw material — calls, notes, past proposals — and we shape it into clear page copy.
Can we edit the site ourselves?
Yes. We set up a CMS so your team can update pages, posts and case studies.
Do you build web apps too?
Yes — client portals, dashboards and internal tools. We scope these separately after a discovery call.
