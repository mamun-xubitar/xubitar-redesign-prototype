About Xubitar

We build software that businesses rely on.

Xubitar Soft is a focused team of engineers working with Shopify merchants, startups and agencies. We take on fewer projects than a typical agency, so each one gets senior attention — and a team that's still there after launch.

Our story

From app studio to long-term technical partner

Xubitar started by building our own Shopify apps. Shipping and supporting software that merchants depend on taught us what actually matters: reliable integrations, honest communication and fast fixes when something breaks.

As the market changed, so did our clients' needs. Many businesses no longer want large agency teams — they want a dependable partner who can solve hard technical problems, automate repetitive work and help them get found. So that's what we focus on today: Shopify development and support, AI automation, search visibility and websites that win clients.

We still build our own products, including XocialAI and XPreOrder. It keeps us close to the reality of running software every day.

How we work

A clear process for every client

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A free 30-minute call to understand the problem, the result you want, timing and budget.

  2. 02

    Written scope

    A clear proposal with deliverables, price, timeline and who is responsible for what.

  3. 03

    Build & preview

    We build on a development or staging store and share progress every week.

  4. 04

    Test & launch

    QA on real devices and data, a launch checklist, and a calm go-live.

  5. 05

    Support & improve

    Documentation, handoff and optional monthly support to keep things running.
