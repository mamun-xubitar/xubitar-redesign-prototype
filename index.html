FAQ

Questions we hear often

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Ask Toki in the chat, or email us directly.

support@xubitar.com
Who do you work with?
Shopify merchants and brands, startups that need a website or automation, and agencies that need a reliable white-label development partner.
How much does a project cost?
It depends on scope. After a short discovery call we send a written proposal with a fixed price and timeline. Ongoing work is covered by monthly support plans.
Do you work white-label with agencies?
Yes. We sign an NDA, work in your tools and under your brand, and never contact your clients unless you ask us to.
Who owns the code?
You do. On final payment you own the code, repositories and credentials, with documentation for future developers.
How quickly do you respond?
We reply to new enquiries within one business day. Support plans include agreed response targets.
Can you guarantee SEO rankings or AI citations?
No one honestly can. We focus on sound technical foundations and useful content, and we report clearly on what changes.

Have a Shopify problem, an automation idea or a website to build?

Tell us what you need. We reply within one business day with honest next steps — no obligation, no hard sell.
